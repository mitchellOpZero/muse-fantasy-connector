FantasyPlug plans

FantasyPlug is a read-only fantasy football companion for Sleeper and ESPN leagues. It never sets lineups, proposes trades, or changes anything in your league.

Free

  • Connect public Sleeper and ESPN leagues
  • Team lists, rosters, weekly matchups, standings, recent transactions

Pro — $8/month

  • Everything in Free, plus unlimited leagues
  • Trade analyzer, waiver targets, and start/sit advice
  • Private ESPN leagues (via your league credentials)

Commissioner — $25/month per league

  • Everything in Pro
  • Weekly recap: results, power rankings, and trade review for your league

Subscriptions are purchased and managed on our website, operatorzero.ai. This page does not sell anything.
