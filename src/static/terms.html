Terms of Service — Fantasy Football Connector

Last updated: September 27, 2026. Operated by Mitchell Moses / OperatorZero.

1. What this is

The Fantasy Football Connector ("the connector") is a read-only agent connector for Meta Muse that lets you view fantasy football leagues hosted on Sleeper, ESPN, and Yahoo, and get heuristic advice about trades, waivers, and lineups. It cannot set lineups, propose or accept trades, add or drop players, or otherwise change anything in your leagues.

2. Accounts and access

Public Sleeper and ESPN leagues require no account. Private ESPN leagues use cookies you provide; Yahoo leagues use OAuth tokens you authorize. Credentials are passed per request from your Secure Credentials Store: the connector is stateless and does not store them. You are responsible for keeping your own credentials safe and for revoking access at the provider (ESPN, Yahoo) if you stop using the connector.

3. Subscriptions and the hosted trial

The hosted connector offers a Free tier (1 league; roster, matchup, standings, and transaction views), a Pro tier ($8/month: unlimited leagues, premium advice tools, private leagues), and a Commissioner tier ($25/month per league: weekly recaps, power rankings, trade review, draft kit). Premium tools return a 402 payment-required response with a subscription URL when your tier does not include them; the connector never charges you itself — payment is handled by the linked checkout provider. Subscriptions are month-to-month and can be cancelled at any time; cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing period.

5. Data

The connector fetches league data from Sleeper, ESPN, and Yahoo on demand and returns it to your agent session. It does not retain league data, credentials, or OAuth tokens between requests. Request logs contain method, path, status, and timing only — never request bodies or credentials. We do not sell personal data.

6. Accuracy

League data comes from third-party providers and may be delayed or incomplete. Advice tools (trade analysis, waiver targets, start/sit) are heuristic and informational only — not professional advice, not a guarantee of outcomes. Always verify consequential decisions in your league's official app.
